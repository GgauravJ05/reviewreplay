Privacy

ReviewReplay is a command line tool that runs on your machine. It has no accounts and sends no analytics or telemetry to its author.

When you run a benchmark, the code under review is sent to the reviewer you configure, such as Claude Code, Codex CLI or Gemini CLI, under that provider's own terms. Results are written to local files you choose.

This website sets no cookies and runs no tracking scripts. Fonts load from Google Fonts and icons from unpkg.

Questions go to the issue tracker.
