Privacy at ReviewReplay

ReviewReplay is a command line tool that runs on your machine. It has no accounts and sends no analytics or telemetry to its author.

When you run a benchmark, the code under review goes to the reviewer you configure, such as Claude Code, Codex CLI or Gemini CLI, under that provider's own terms. Results are written to local files you choose.

This website sets no cookies and runs no tracking scripts. Fonts load from Google Fonts; everything else is served from this site.

Questions go to the issue tracker.
